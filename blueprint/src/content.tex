\section{Virtual Double Categories}

A quiver span $R$ is a span $Q \xrightarrow{} R \xleftarrow{} S$ between two quivers, but axiomatized via its fibers.

We will think of vertices as objects, of edges in the two quivers as proarrows and of the cells as filling a square of arrows and proarrows.

\begin{definition}
    \lean{CategoryTheory.QuiverSpan}
    A quiver span between quivers $Q$ and $R$ consists of the following data: First, a type $R(x,y)$ of arrows for each vertex $x$ in $Q$ and for each vertex $y$ in $R$. Then, for each pair of edges $e : x \to x'$ and $e' : y \to y'$ in $Q$ and $R$, respectively and for arrows $f \in R(x,y)$ and $f' \in R(x',y')$, a type of cells $R(e,e',f,f')$.
\end{definition}
